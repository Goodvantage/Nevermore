# Nevermore privacy

Nevermore connects directly to the Raven/Frappe sites you configure. Browser login is handled by the site's system-browser page. The app receives OAuth tokens; it does not collect your site password. Tokens are kept in account-scoped Keychain items. Requests carrying account credentials are confined to that account's origin, including redirects and private downloads.

Account metadata, received history, drafts and queued sends are kept locally in the app's sandbox. Received history is bounded and can be cleared or disabled in Settings. Drafts and accepted queued sends are retained for recovery until sent or explicitly discarded; removing an account discards them after a warning. Window routes, frames, saved searches, reading preferences and account-scoped local quiet hours are stored in app preferences. Removing an account also clears its saved window routes, saved searches and quiet hours. Local databases and files are not encrypted by an app-specific encryption layer. FileVault and your Mac's access controls provide the device-at-rest boundary. Backups and exported attachments are outside guaranteed removal.

Nevermore does not include analytics, advertising, crash uploads, Spotlight indexing, iCloud database/token synchronization or a mandatory Nevermore-operated relay. No message content is sent to an AI service by this client. Features your Raven site or bots provide remain subject to that site's policies.

Promised files are received into a private temporary destination, copied into protected snapshots, and staged under the originating account. Account changes cancel uncommitted imports. Temporary destinations are cleaned on completion/cancellation and graceful Quit; abandoned marked destinations are checked on startup. Providers can continue writing after cancellation, so forced termination and a broken provider can delay cleanup.

Reading/clipboard text omits hidden spoiler bodies and the editor’s original-HTML metadata. Code-reader windows close and clear when their source is deleted/changed, the account changes, or known parent permissions are revoked.

The expanded Markdown and table editors work locally. They do not send text to
an editor service or fetch images embedded in source. Their unapplied edits stay
in memory; Use Text/Use Table updates the normal draft, and Cancel discards the
transaction. Account changes and window closure dismiss attached private editors.

Private images are fetched through the authenticated site transport. When a profile photo is unavailable, Nevermore draws a raven portrait locally from a stable hash of the Raven username, not the display name. Channel tiles use a separate name-and-workspace seed; renaming a channel changes its colors and pattern, while access level changes its hash or lock symbol. It sends no names to an avatar service and writes no generated image to disk. The bounded generated-image caches belong to the account and are cleared when that account stops. Arbitrary remote HTML, scripts, embeds and tracking images are not executed as browser content, and remote images other than GIF service GIFs are not loaded. Opening an external link sends it to your selected browser/application, whose privacy policy then applies.

GIFs in messages are images hosted by a GIF service (KLIPY, GIPHY or Tenor), the way Raven's web client sends them. Nevermore loads such a GIF itself, from that service only, over the same checked, cookie-free transport as link previews: no Raven account, token or cookie goes with the request, and the service sees your network address and when you read the message. Settings ▸ GIFs turns this off; the GIF's description is shown instead. Images in messages from anywhere else are never loaded. Decoded GIFs stay in memory and are not written to disk.

GIF search (Conversation ▸ Send GIF…) sends what you type, a page number, a content rating and your language to the GIF service you chose, from this Mac, with your own key for that service. KLIPY also receives the region set on this Mac and a random identifier made on this Mac, which it asks for to tell its users apart; the identifier is not linked to your Raven account. The key is kept in your Keychain and Nevermore ships none. A GIF you send is not uploaded to your Raven site: the message links the GIF on the service, as the terms of both search services require, so everyone who reads it loads it from there. That service's privacy policy applies to these requests.

Chat notifications are opt-in and use generic message bodies. They run through the live app's connection; sleep or Quit stops this delivery path. No APNs/FCM provider enrollment is included. Local quiet hours and Do Not Disturb suppress incoming notifications. Presence refreshes only from interaction with Nevermore while it is active; quitting does not clear presence established by another device. Notification activation reopens the matching local account and conversation.

Software update checks contact apps.goodvantage.cloud about ten seconds after launch and daily while Nevermore runs. They are enabled by default and can be disabled in Settings → General; manual checks still work. Prerelease builds read the beta manifest, other builds read stable. The separate, cookie-free transport sends the User-Agent `Nevermore/<version> (macOS)`, but no account, token, document data or device identifier. The host sees your network address. Redirects are refused. Demos and automated fixtures do not check automatically.

A newer release can prompt for system notification permission. Notification attempts and dismissed versions are remembered locally so relaunching does not repeat the notice. Update notifications contain only the public version. Download opens the validated installer URL in your browser, whose download/privacy rules then apply. Nevermore does not download, execute or install updates itself; Sparkle is not started.

Home's "Your Week" and "You Talk with Most" are worked out on this Mac from the times and conversations of messages you sent in the last 30 days, which your Raven site lists only for you. Nevermore does not store or upload these numbers, and nothing on Home describes how other people use Raven. Showing Home does not mark conversations or threads read.

Search sends what you type in the search field to your Raven site after a short pause, so a search refined word by word reaches the site several times. A search in one conversation or workspace also asks the site which threads it has, so their replies can be included. Search shows matching messages saved on this Mac at once; these never leave it. Previewing a result loads a few messages around it and does not mark anything read. Searches are kept only when you save them, on this Mac, for that account.

Diagnostic errors use endpoint/error categories and identifiers rather than chat content or credentials. Do not include private chat histories, authentication callbacks, tokens or full databases in public issue reports.
